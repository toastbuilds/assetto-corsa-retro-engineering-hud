# Retro UI 2.8.0

This is a big update for Retro UI: build a GT7 cockpit that suits your setup, follow race events more easily, and see clearer timing and telemetry across the HUD.

## Make the cockpit yours

- **Dash:** a new horizontal telemetry window gives you two layouts for the readings you want in view while driving.
- **GT7 side gauges:** choose from 16 mini-gauge families, with separate style selections for the speed and RPM sides.
- **A live slipstream glow:** GT7 speed dials now show a soft amber glow when your car gets a tow. It follows the focused car and fades naturally as the draft changes.
- **Lighting that feels at home:** GT7 dials and their smaller gauges illuminate smoothly with the car's headlights, while keeping readings legible.
- **Your own colors in every app:** choose a theme and palette per HUD app, so the Leaderboard, Timer and instruments can each have their own style.

## Follow the race

- **Race Notices tell the story of an overtake:** see a short recap with the rival's recent flags and track exits. An optional graph shows how the gap changed over the last four laps.
- **Suggested corner gear:** get a cue for the gear that suits the next corner, with controls to place it where it works best in your layout.
- **Layered pedal traces:** compare what you pressed with the throttle or brake the car received. ABS and traction-control activity remain visible in the output.
- **A clearer Leaderboard:** session time and lap progress have more room, connection quality is easier to spot online, and estimated lap times are marked with `~`. Air and track temperatures have been removed to make space for the useful race information.

## More dependable timing

- Timed races now recognize the last lap, so the final result is captured when the session ends.
- The Timer clock stays centered, and sector times and deltas keep a consistent text size as values change.
- Fuel and boost needles, low-fuel warnings, RPM redline marks and crowded gauge labels have also been refined.

## Install

Download **RetroUI-v2.8.0.zip**, drag it into Content Manager, install and enable Retro UI. Manual installation extracts the ZIP into the Assetto Corsa folder. Existing `apps/lua/RetroUI` installations and saved settings are retained.

Retro UI remains free, with every feature included. See the [user guide](https://github.com/yukkine0704/assetto-corsa-retro-engineering-hud/blob/v2.8.0/docs/user-guide.md), [settings reference](https://github.com/yukkine0704/assetto-corsa-retro-engineering-hud/blob/v2.8.0/docs/settings.md) and [full changelog](https://github.com/yukkine0704/assetto-corsa-retro-engineering-hud/blob/v2.8.0/CHANGELOG.md).
