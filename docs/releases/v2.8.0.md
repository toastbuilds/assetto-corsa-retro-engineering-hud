# Retro UI 2.8.0

Fresh colors and themes, a new Dash, and more ways to style your gauges.

- **Colors & themes:** pick a palette and theme for each app.
- **New Dash:** choose an RPM bar above or a side tachometer. Both show speed, gear, fuel, boost and pedals.
- **New styles:** 16 GT7 mini-gauges, with separate styles for speed and RPM. Speed dials glow when you catch a draft.
- **On track:** corner gear hints, pedal traces, overtake recaps and an optional four-lap gap graph.
- **Clearer screens:** the Leaderboard puts session time and lap progress first. The Timer catches the final lap and keeps sector/delta text steady.
- **Polish:** clearer fuel and redline warnings, smoother GT7 lighting, and online connection bars.

Drop **RetroUI-v2.8.0.zip** into Content Manager.
